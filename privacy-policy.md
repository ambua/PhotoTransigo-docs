# PhotoTransigo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

## Summary

PhotoTransigo works entirely on your device. It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account, no telemetry, no analytics, and no advertising.

## What the app does

PhotoTransigo copies photos and videos from a connected phone or a folder on your PC into date-organized folders on your PC. All processing happens locally; no file content or metadata ever leaves your device.

## Local log file

To help diagnose reported problems, the app writes a log file to its local app-data folder on your device. The log may contain:

- folder paths you selected as source or target,
- file names and paths of files that failed to import,
- the name of a connected device,
- app version, timestamps, and error messages.

The log never leaves your device. The app does not upload it or send it anywhere. It exists only so that *you* can attach it to a bug report if you choose to. Only the five newest daily log files are kept; older ones are deleted automatically. You can delete the log files at any time by deleting them in the log folder or by uninstalling the app.

## Permissions

- **File system access**: the app reads and writes only the folders you explicitly pick as source and target.
- **Portable device (removable storage) access**: used to read photos and videos from a phone you connect, so they can be copied to your PC.

## Third parties

The app does not use third-party services and shares nothing with anyone.
